Metric Movement Explainer

A metric moved. Was it real, and what caused it? Pick two periods and the tool finds the segments that explain the change.

Data

Needs a date column and a revenue column. One row per order. Other text columns become dimensions.

Question

Use periods of equal length that cover whole weeks. Then each day is compared with the same weekday.

Result

Load a dataset and press “Explain the change”.

Which dimension is the odd one?

Surprise measures how much the split of the metric across this dimension changed. A larger value means a more unusual shift. “Explained by top” is the share of the change covered by the few segments that moved most.

Does the tool find the truth?

We inject a known cause into fake orders, run the tool, and check whether it finds it. About 15 runs per case, around 15 seconds.
